家長／老師批改用 — 標「依實際情況」的題目,孩子照真實情況寫即可,句型對就給分。
Homework: Write the past tense sentence.
  1. They were at the park last weekend. (Tim and Billy were... 亦可)
  2. You and your friend were tired in class last Monday. (We were tired in class last Monday. 亦可)
Homework: Answer the questions in complete sentences.
  1. Yes, they were in the kitchen ten minutes ago. (the girls → they)
  2. No, we weren't scared of the dog last night. (you and your brother → we)
  3. Yes, they were at the basketball game last weekend. (Billy and Tim → they)
  4. No, they weren't in Taipei last month. (grandmother and grandfather → they)
Homework: Write the question.
  1. Were you and Ian at the tennis game two days ago? ( No ) (x - we → you and Ian)
  2. Were Pata and Daniel excited about the weekend? ( Yes ) (x - they → Pata and Daniel)
Level 5 Review
  1. Yes, we play volleyball on the weekend.
  2. No, they don't read comics in class.
Family and Friends: Unit 2
  1. Yes, I like playing tennis. / No, I don't like playing tennis. (依實際情況)
  2. Yes, she likes taking photos. (三單 likes + ing)
  3. No, they don't like doing gymnastics.
  4. Yes, they like playing volleyball.
  5. She likes reading comics. (take photos 型:動詞改 ing)
批改重點 Grading focus: ① 複數主詞(Tim and Rosy / the girls / you and...)過去式用 were,否定 weren't ② 回答時主詞要替換:the girls → they;you and your friend → we ③ 寫問句:( x - we ) 換成 you and Ian、( x - they ) 換成 Pata and Daniel,句尾問號 + ( Yes / No ) ④ like + 動詞ing(like playing / doing / taking / reading),三單用 likes ⑤ 寫錯的句子抄到 Corrections 區訂正一次。
