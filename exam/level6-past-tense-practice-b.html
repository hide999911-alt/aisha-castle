A)  I am in the kitchen now. ( yesterday )
I was in the kitchen yesterday.
C)  Were you at the park yesterday? ( Yes )

B)  You are tired now. ( last night )
You were tired last night.
D)  Was I hungry last week? ( No )

Answer Key 參考答案

Homework: Write the past tense sentence.
  1. I was at my cousin's house last Sunday.
  2. You were in the kitchen twenty minutes ago.
Homework: Answer the questions in complete sentences.
  1. No, I wasn't in my mother's car yesterday.
  2. I was at home last Sunday. (答案可不同,例如 at the park / in Hsinchu)
  3. No, you weren't happy in class last week.
  4. Yes, you were in Taipei last month.
Homework: Write the question.
  1. Were you in the living room ten minutes ago? ( No )
  2. Was I angry yesterday? ( Yes )
Level 5 Review
  1. No, I don't drink milk in my bedroom. (依實際情況,Yes, I drink milk in my bedroom. 亦可)
  2. Yes, you speak English in class.
Family and Friends: Starter
  1. My English teacher's name is __________. (依實際情況)
  2. There are __________ pencils in my pencil box. (依實際情況,注意用 There are)
  3. He wants to __________ on Sunday. (例如 He wants to watch TV on Sunday. 注意 wants 加 s)
  4. are — There are thirty cows on the farm.
  5. It is __________ in Taipei. (例如 It is sunny / raining in Taipei.)
批改重點 Grading focus: ① was / were 與主詞搭配(I・he・she → was;you・we・they → were) ② 時間詞位置與 now → yesterday / last... / ...ago 的替換 ③ 回答要用完整句子,句首大寫、句尾句點 ④ 寫錯的句子請孩子抄到 Corrections 區訂正一次。
