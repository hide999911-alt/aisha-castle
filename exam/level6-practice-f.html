家長／老師批改用 — 標「依實際情況」的題目,孩子照真實情況寫即可,句型對就給分。
Homework: Write the past tense sentence.
  1. They were at the tennis game two days ago. (Rosy and Lillian were... 亦可)
  2. You and your brother were hungry at home last night. (We were hungry at home last night. 亦可)
Homework: Answer the questions in complete sentences.
  1. Yes, they were at the volleyball game last Saturday. (the boys → they)
  2. No, we weren't in the classroom two hours ago. (you and your friend → we)
  3. Yes, they were worried about the test yesterday. (Billy and Rosy → they)
  4. No, they weren't in the kitchen five minutes ago. (mother and father → they)
Homework: Write the question.
  1. Were you and Ian happy at the chess game last week? ( No ) (x - we → you and Ian)
  2. Were Pata and Daniel in the garden one hour ago? ( Yes ) (x - they → Pata and Daniel)
Level 5 Review
  1. No, we don't take photos at the park.
  2. Yes, they play basketball on Fridays.
Family and Friends: Unit 2
  1. Yes, I like playing basketball. / No, I don't like playing basketball. (依實際情況)
  2. No, he doesn't like playing chess. (三單否定 doesn't like + ing)
  3. Yes, they like reading comic books.
  4. No, they don't like riding skateboards.
  5. He likes watching TV. (watch TV 型:動詞改 ing)
批改重點 Grading focus: ① 複數主詞(Tim and Rosy / the girls / you and...)過去式用 were,否定 weren't ② 回答時主詞要替換:the girls → they;you and your friend → we ③ 寫問句:( x - we ) 換成 you and Ian、( x - they ) 換成 Pata and Daniel,句尾問號 + ( Yes / No ) ④ like + 動詞ing(like playing / doing / taking / reading),三單用 likes ⑤ 寫錯的句子抄到 Corrections 區訂正一次。
