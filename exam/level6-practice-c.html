家長／老師批改用 — 標「依實際情況」的題目,孩子照真實情況寫即可,句型對就給分。
Homework: Write the past tense sentence.
  1. My aunt was in Australia last month. (She was in Australia last month. 亦可)
  2. Rosy was excited about the party yesterday. (She was excited about the party yesterday. 亦可)
Homework: Answer the questions in complete sentences.
  1. No, it wasn't cold in Spain last winter.
  2. Yes, it was sunny in Thailand yesterday.
  3. Yes, he was asleep in the living room ten minutes ago. (Billy → he)
  4. No, he wasn't in the garden five minutes ago. (No, my father wasn't... 亦可)
Homework: Write the question.
  1. Was Lillian in the forest two days ago? ( No ) (x - she → 用 Lillian)
  2. Was Ian awake last night? ( Yes ) (x - he → 用 Ian)
Level 5 Review
  1. No, she doesn't see Billy in the garden.
  2. Yes, he reads books on Sundays. (注意三單動詞加 s:reads)
Family and Friends: Unit 1
  1. I'm from Taiwan. (依實際情況;國名 Taiwan 開頭要大寫)
  2. Yes, she is from Vietnam. / No, she isn't from Vietnam. (依實際情況)
  3. No, she isn't from Mexico.
  4. She is from __________. (依實際情況,例如 She is from America. / He is from Australia.)
  5. He is nine years old.
批改重點 Grading focus: ① was / wasn't 與主詞、時間詞搭配(now → yesterday / last... / ...ago) ② 寫問句時 ( x - she ) 換成 Lillian、( x - he ) 換成 Ian,句尾要有問號和 ( Yes / No ) ③ Level 5 Review 是現在式:三單動詞加 s(sees / reads),否定用 doesn't ④ 國名開頭大寫(Taiwan、Mexico...) ⑤ 寫錯的句子抄到 Corrections 區訂正一次。
